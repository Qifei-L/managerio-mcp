# Inventory, costing and landed cost

Part of the Evertrust Books accounting skill. The core rules in SKILL.md always apply.

## Inventory control accounts and items

- Use `list_inventory_control_accounts`, `get_inventory_control_account`, `create_inventory_control_account`, and `update_inventory_control_account` for the native `/control-account-for-inventory-items-form`. These are special control accounts, not ordinary Balance Sheet accounts. Deletion is not supported.
- Listing probes only IDs in the complete company chart and reports failed probes separately. `complete` describes that chart-backed result; `inventory_complete:false` means unused accounts absent from the chart cannot be enumerated. Do not claim complete native control-account inventory or silently treat failed probes as absent accounts.
- Creation requires the requested Name and an evidenced Balance Sheet Group ID. Read the destination group and its ancestors; use built-in roots only from returned native IDs. The tool checks duplicates against the whole chart, validates the parent, and reads back. Updates require the current `expected_hash`, merge requested fields, preserve other settings, and invalidate COA snapshots after accepted writes. Never repeat an uncertain create/update.
- Link inventory items to a control account only through the item's evidenced native control field using `update_record(resource:"inventory_items")`. A prefix such as Vehicles in ItemName is a naming convention, not a control-account assignment. Read the item and target control account first and verify the stored link after editing.
- Use `get_resource_schema` to inspect this company's advertised form request schemas and their referenced definitions. Generic object schemas do not prove field names or enum values. If no template or schema establishes the inventory control/item/document link, report it unresolved instead of inventing JSON fields.
- Selecting an inventory item on an invoice can change quantities, asset postings, and future cost of sales. Obtain the user's inventory-entry instruction, read the full invoice, preserve dates, supplier, reference, gross amount and VAT, and replace the full Lines array only after its inventory-link fields are evidenced. Verify both storage and native inventory quantities/cost; a description/Qty change on account 1036 alone does not create stock.
- Check quantity multiplied by unit price against the original printed subtotal. Retain enough unit-price precision to preserve the approved total; do not silently change invoice principal/VAT or add a rounding journal.

## Inventory costing capability

- `inspect_inventory_costing` distinguishes stored API2 unit-cost records from the native Recalculate action. The bounded unit-cost response is one page only. A unit-cost form or costing worksheet is not proof of an automated Recalculate endpoint.
- API2 catalogue absence means automatic recalculation remains Pending. Forum reports of `/api4/inventory-unit-cost-batch` do not prove its request body or API2-token authentication. Do not infer API4 sessions, write speculative payloads, silently calculate your own cost algorithm, or replace native recalculation with a journal.
- Native recalculation requires an evidenced action and explicit company/item/period scope, preserving Manager's lock date and valuation method. Verify quantities, stock value and COGS after the operation; stored invoice Item links alone prove quantity movement, not cost posting.

- Browser fallback was tested in Sample Use: Inventory Items → Recalculate → Inventory Cost Correction, inspect the proposed item/date/unit cost rows, then Apply Changes for the authorized scope. Observe the actual current UI rather than guessing URLs or API4 payloads. Read quantities/value and invoice costOfSales before and after. Sample evidence: buy 10 at 10, sell 2; native action preserved quantity 8 and changed inventory value 100→80 and sales COGS null→20. This proves the UI workflow, not an MCP recalculation endpoint. Keep test fixtures separate from real company records.

## Landed cost: source correction, allocation and native completion

1. Establish the authorized company, period, batch and cost policy from original invoices, customs declarations, contracts and goods purchases. Separate recoverable VAT from cost. Costs that do not qualify for capitalization remain expenses; resolve storage, cleaning or demurrage treatment from their nature and the user's evidenced policy, rather than the account label alone.
2. Reconcile existing goods quantities, item keys, purchase values, sales and opening balances first. Keep distinct models distinct and preserve explicitly unassigned items. Search current records and resume saved keys after interruption; never create a second invoice, opening or allocation to repeat a test.
3. Use the authorized ordinary asset account for Landed Cost - Pending Allocation. Correct each original bill, payment or journal directly to that asset account, preserving date, counterparty, tax, gross amount, settlement and unrelated lines. Do not post an expense-offsetting reclassification journal to fix the original account unless the correction rule in the core skill allows it. Account-to-report conversion is not supported by ordinary COA tools; do not fabricate it. Any required new account still needs the explicit creation authorization described below.
4. Prepare an external source-to-batch schedule and an allocation basis supported by the goods. A simple purchase-value allocation uses each product's batch purchase value divided by that batch's total; preserve currency precision and distribute cent remainders deterministically so every batch ties exactly. Do not allocate old COGS again or include duplicate invoice payments. Do not silently backdate costs or assume late charges retrospectively change earlier sales; preserve the authorized recognition date and inspect native costing effects.
5. Test one real authorized allocation first. In Journal Entries debit native Inventory on hand, choose the exact Item on each debit and leave Qty blank to add value only; credit the asset landed-cost clearing account. Use actual native fields learned from the current same-company form. This is an allocation of an asset already recorded, not a reversing correction entry. Do not invent Qty/Item enablement flags or use Qty 1 as a workaround. Keep calculation/evidence logs outside business descriptions.
6. Read back the source and allocation. Verify original principal/VAT/gross/settlement, exact subsidiary links, allocated amounts, unchanged item quantities, clearing debit/credit and native inventory value against complete GL. A successful stored journal is not completed costing. Reconcile inventory plus COGS changes to the amount allocated and explain every remaining clearing balance.
7. Use native Inventory Items → Recalculate for the authorized scope when the current deployed interface supports it; inspect every proposed create/update/delete with item, date, old/new value and action. Preserve valuation method and lock date. Execute only authorized native changes. After Apply Changes, reopen the native correction screen and reread the proposals. One application may leave further actions, especially removal of old unit-cost records. Record the remaining action count and exact records, not merely that Apply Changes was clicked.
8. Completion requires a fresh native queue with no applicable pending actions, together with unchanged quantities, reconciled inventory/COGS, clearing balances and unchanged unrelated tax/cash/AP/AR. Button state is supporting UI evidence only; an available navigation link or zero duplicate count is not a substitute for the native queue. If more actions remain, review the changed proposal before another authorized pass. Stop on repeated identical proposals, failed deletions, ambiguous old records, concurrency, unknown results or three unsuccessful passes; retain a checkpoint and report the remaining work rather than looping blindly.
9. Use get_inventory_cost_state to read complete bounded inventory/unit-cost records and expose duplicate item/date keys. It cannot read the native correction queue or establish completion: native_recalculation_complete and pending_native_actions remain unknown. For a native-evidenced same-item/date duplicate, prepare_inventory_cost_cleanup accepts explicit removed/retained keys and both current hashes, preparing only a durable preview with retained-record guards. Review exact permanent deletions before apply_record_changes; a preview/hash does not grant authorization. Never select the newest record or highest cost as an automatic winner. Other obsolete dates must use their separately evidenced native workflow.
10. After cleanup inspect the saved task, confirm absence of deleted records and unchanged retained records, reread complete relevant state and GL, then check the native queue again. API2 unit-cost CRUD is not the Recalculate algorithm. If the connection does not advertise a verified native action, report that limitation and use the authorized native UI; do not infer API4 credentials, scrape hidden application state, invent payloads or calculate a replacement valuation algorithm. Separate source implementation, synthetic tests, native live verification and deployment in the result.

Official references: [freight-in allocation](https://www2.manager.io/guides/9610) and [native batch correction completion and lock date](https://forum.manager.io/t/added-ability-to-batch-recalculate-inventory-costs/60839). Native UI/schema evidence for the current version takes precedence over historical field names in guides.
